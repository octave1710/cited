# Are you in the answer, or is someone else?

## What changed

People used to search and pick from a list of links. More and more, they ask an assistant
and read one answer. That answer names a handful of sources. You are either one of them or
you are not, and there is no second page to be on.

The loss is invisible in your reporting. A click that never happens leaves no trace in
analytics and none in the ad platforms, so nothing in the monthly deck says it happened.

## What this tool does

**It asks the assistants your customers' questions and records who they credit.** Six of
them: Google's AI Overview, Google's AI Mode, Perplexity, ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude. Not a
prediction, not a model's opinion. The real answers and the real sources behind them.

You type a category. It writes about 160 questions a buyer of that category actually asks,
puts a panel of them to all six, and shows you, question by question, which sites they
credit and whether yours is ever one.

Then it does three more things.

**It explains why the winners win**, using nothing but those citations. On one real run the
site the assistants reached for was named first far more often than anyone else, while the
site with the most mentions overall was never named first once. Those are different
problems and they need different work.

**It scores one of your pages** against the things that make content quotable, and writes
the actual rewrites, ranked. Not "improve clarity". The sentence you have and the sentence
to replace it with. Where a rewrite needs a real figure or a named expert, it does not
invent one; it asks you for it on a form.

**It drafts content for several markets and refuses to publish it** until a named person on
your team has approved each market. That refusal is in the machinery, not a checkbox.

## What you get to keep

A spreadsheet of every citation, every question, every source. A list of the questions where
you are absent and who took them. Your page with the safe fixes already written in. The
structured data to paste. A brief written from your own numbers.

## What it is worth

Running a category costs a little over a cent and takes about a minute. Putting a panel of
questions to all six engines costs about a quarter of a dollar and takes four minutes. That
is cheap enough to run on a prospect before you meet them.

The work it points at is ordered by how cheap it is to win, not by how big the target is.
Displacing the site that wins almost everything is a year of work. Taking the seat of a site
that wins nowhere else is one page.

## What it does not do

It measures who the assistants credit today, and it can show a page moving from ignored to
quoted after the edits. It does not control what any assistant indexes, and it will not
promise you a position. Where a number is missing it leaves the gap visible and asks you,
rather than filling it in.

Everything on screen can be checked without us. Retype a question into an assistant. Open
the robots.txt file. Read the sentence we quoted on the page we quoted it from.
