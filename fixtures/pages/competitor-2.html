We Tested 12 Vitamin C Serums for 90 Days

We put 12 popular vitamin C serums through a 90-day test with weekly photo tracking. 7 of 12 showed visible brightening by week 8, and 3 oxidized badly within the first month.

How we tested

Each editor used one serum daily for 90 days under the same SPF 50 routine. We photographed under standardized lighting weekly and tracked texture, brightness, and irritation.

What actually worked

The serums that performed best all had three things in common: 15% concentration or close to it, opaque airless packaging, and a pH between 3.0 and 3.5. Price did not predict performance; our second-best performer costs $19.

What to avoid

Dropper bottles in clear glass consistently oxidized by week 4. Anything that arrives already yellow-orange has lost most of its potency before first use.
