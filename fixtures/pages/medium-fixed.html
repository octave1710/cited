Vitamin C Serum Guide

In this guide we will walk you through everything you need to know about vitamin C serum, one of the most popular skincare ingredients on the market today. Whether you are new to skincare or a seasoned enthusiast, there is something here for you.

What are the benefits of vitamin C serum?

"Vitamin C is the rare active where the marketing claim and the trial data mostly agree. The catch is that nothing visible happens before week eight," says Dr. Sofia Reyes, board-certified dermatologist.

Daily 10-20% vitamin C reduced hyperpigmentation scores by 29% within 12 weeks across 31 published trials (2023 meta-analysis, PubMed 37298401). In those trials, visible brightening appeared at 8 weeks and collagen-related change was measurable from week 12. A 2026 Copenhagen protocol pairing 15% vitamin C with 0.3% retinol measured a 31% wrinkle-depth reduction in 16 weeks.

Mid-range concentrations are a popular choice for most people, though everyone's skin is different.

How to use it

Apply a few drops to clean skin in the morning before your moisturizer and sunscreen. Consistency matters more than quantity, so a daily routine is recommended. Some people prefer to use it at night instead, which is also fine.

What to look for when buying

Look for quality packaging, since vitamin C can degrade over time when exposed to the elements. There are several forms of vitamin C used in serums, and some may be gentler than others if you have sensitive skin, so it is worth doing your research.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use vitamin C with retinol?

Most dermatologists suggest using vitamin C in the morning and retinol at night rather than layering them together.
